Clear, informed decisions about how you eat

I help clients make clear, informed decisions about how they eat and understand why those decisions matter. My work draws on over fifteen years of studying the relationship between nutrition, biology, and behaviour.

My background is in psychology, biology, and microbiology, studied in the UK. I'm currently completing a PhD at the First Faculty of Medicine, Charles University in Prague, where I study the relationship between our environment and mental health.

I also trained intensively in whole-food cooking at a specialist culinary institute in Belgium, where I developed practical chef skills that inform how I think about food in real kitchens.

How I work with clients

01

An initial conversation

A complimentary 20-minute call to understand your situation and determine whether my approach is the right fit. I'll be candid about whether I can help.

02

A comprehensive assessment

A detailed look at your health history, current dietary patterns, lifestyle, and goals. I consider the full context, including the psychological and cultural factors that influence how you eat.

03

A bespoke plan with ongoing support

You receive a tailored nutritional strategy with the reasoning behind every recommendation clearly explained. We continue working together, refining and adjusting as your needs evolve.
